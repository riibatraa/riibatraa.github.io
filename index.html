Research

the weaponization of transparency

Trust Issues — a final-year thesis on the weaponization of transparency, consumer trust and influencer credibility in beauty. Mixed-methods, primary data, built around four brands that each solve the trust problem differently: Fae Beauty, The Ordinary, Indē Wild and Huda Beauty.

Transparency only works when it feels human

Overly scientific or fear-based communication alienates rather than reassures. Restraint reads as integrity; volume reads as a sales pitch.

Influencers translate complexity

They convert data into narrative, simplifying what brands overcomplicate. That translation is the product — and it's why credibility sits with the person, not the label.

Emotional trust beats informational trust

Consumers engage with sincerity more than they respond to evidence. We don't understand ingredients so much as recognise them, and recognition feels like knowledge.
